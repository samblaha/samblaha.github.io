## Overview
My golf ball printer was designed from an egg printer. I made some CAD adjustments and redesigned the drawing arm to fit the size of a golf ball.

## How it works
The printer is controlled by an Inkscape bot. You upload an image, SVG, or text, and it gets transformed into a path the printer can follow and draw onto the ball.
