## Overview
Retro Pi is a Raspberry Pi turned into a living-room console. Raspbian is the OS, EmulationStation is the menu on the TV, and RetroArch runs the emulators. Arcade, older consoles, and classic PC games all live on the same small box.

## The box
The hardware is a Pi in a vented white case, HDMI to a TV, USB for controllers. Two SNES-style iNNEXT pads sit on the case; HDMI and USB are plugged in, status LED on. No extra display, no custom cabinet — the TV is the screen, the pads are USB, and the Pi is the computer.

EmulationStation is what you browse. Pick a system, pick a game. RetroArch sits behind that and runs the cores, so the same box can jump from an arcade title to a console game without swapping hardware. Raspbian is what boots and holds the stack together.

## The library
This build has hundreds of games on it — arcade, console, and classic PC. The range is the point: Mario Kart on the Nintendo 64 on one side, Galaga on a classic arcade machine on the other. Sit down, pick a title, play it on a real screen with real pads. Mario Kart 64 on a TV is the typical session.
