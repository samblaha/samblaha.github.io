## Overview
Retro Pi is a classic game emulator built on Raspbian, EmulationStation, and RetroArch. It lets you play your favorite arcade, older console, and classic PC games from one small box.

## The library
This build has hundreds of games on it, everything from Mario Kart on the Nintendo 64 to Galaga on a classic arcade machine.
