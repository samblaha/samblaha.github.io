# Little-world surface asset

`moss-rock-albedo.png` was generated with the built-in Image Gen tool for the rotatable Three.js scene in `assets/js/garage3d/planet-world.js`.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: seamless physically based color texture for a 3D rocky miniature planet. Generate ONE square 2048 texture tile, orthographic straight down, uniformly lit, no shadows or ambient lighting baked in. Entire frame a continuous natural dark slate-gray fractured granite surface with fine rough mineral grain, irregular fissures and rock variation, 35 percent densely covered by living olive green moss and tiny fern-like ground cover creeping along cracks in patches. Photoreal microtexture, lifelike moss tufts, realistic subtle dark charcoal cool grey rock. No boulders in perspective, no scene, no sphere, no planet, no text, no labels, no borders, no UI. Seamlessly tileable across all four edges. This will be the albedo map on the real rotatable 3D model.

The actual delivered image is 1254 × 1254. It is used as a repeating surface map, never as a screenshot substitute. Geometry, lights, navigation, shadows, and flowing water are rendered live.
