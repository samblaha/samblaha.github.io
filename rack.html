Courses played

Posted scores on this rack come from a local scorebook in the site data — not a live GHIN feed. GHIN does not offer a public scores API (club/vendor licenses only; public lookup is handicap ID, not scorecards). When a round is posted, add it to rack-scores.js and it shows on the matching ball.
